# Postloom privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 September 2026*

Postloom is a desktop app made by **R2DSolutions**. It sends personal emails to
a list of people, from your own email account. This policy explains what
information Postloom handles and where it goes.

**In short:** everything stays on your computer. R2DSolutions does not collect,
receive, sell or share any of your information, and Postloom has no analytics,
advertising or tracking.

## What Postloom keeps on your computer

To do its job, Postloom stores the following in a database on your computer, in
your user account's app-data folder:

- **Your email account settings:** the address, server name and port, and the
  sender names you choose.
- **Your templates**, including any pictures you add to them.
- **The lists of people you send to:** the spreadsheet rows you choose,
  including names, email addresses and any other details in those rows.
- **History:** who each send went to, and whether each email was sent.
- **Your settings**, and daily **backups** of all of the above.

**Your email password** (or app password) is encrypted with a key that your
computer's own secure password store holds (Windows data protection, the macOS
Keychain, or the Linux secret service). Only the encrypted password is kept in
Postloom's database; it is never put in backups or diagnostics, and it can't be
read on another computer or by another user account.

None of this is sent to R2DSolutions.

## What leaves your computer

Postloom connects to the internet only:

- **To your email provider** (for example Gmail or Outlook), to send the emails
  you ask it to send. This uses the account and server you set up, over an
  encrypted connection (TLS). Your provider's own privacy policy covers what
  happens to those emails.
- **To GitHub, once a day, to check for a new version** (only in the versions
  downloaded from GitHub; not in the Microsoft Store version, which the Store
  updates). This check sends no information about you or your data. You can turn
  it off in Settings → About.

When you open a help link or a download page, it opens in your web browser.

## Diagnostics

If you choose **Export diagnostics** in Help, Postloom saves a file to your
computer describing the app version and how it is set up. It contains no
passwords, email addresses, list contents or email content. The file is only
shared if you send it to someone yourself.

## Your control over your information

- You can see and change everything Postloom keeps from within the app.
- **Settings → Your data** lets you choose how long History is kept, clear it,
  take a backup or restore one, and open the folder where Postloom keeps its
  data.
- Deleting an email account in Senders & accounts deletes its saved password.
- Uninstalling Postloom and deleting its data folder removes everything it kept.

## Security

Passwords are encrypted with a key from your operating system's password store, emails are sent
over encrypted connections only, and Postloom's database stays on your computer.
Anyone who can use your computer while you are signed in could open Postloom's
data, so we recommend a password on your computer and disk encryption
(BitLocker or Device encryption on Windows, FileVault on macOS).

## Children

Postloom is a business tool and is not directed at children.
